Privacy

This site is made and run by me, Kieron Oates. I don't use cookies and I don't track you.

Your location

If you choose to show your sky, your location is used only inside your browser to work out what's above you. It is saved in your browser's local storage so you don't have to set it again, and it is never sent to me or anyone else. You can clear it at any time with "Forget this place" in the sky panel, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Visitor numbers

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies or store anything on your device, and it doesn't identify you.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. I don't have access to it in any form that identifies you.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, you can reach me through my portfolio site.
